\chapter{Discussion}
\label{ch:discussion}

This chapter answers the research questions (Section~\ref{sec:intro-requirements}), interprets the metrics and
states the limitations.

\section{Answers to the research questions}
\label{sec:disc-requirements}

\paragraph{Real-image supervision.}
The converted 3DPW labels, whose joints lie a median \DataCheckJointsNew\,mm from those of 3DPW, enable training
and testing Anny models on real images, but \DataConstantCount{} phenotypes are constant and head and hands
remain at rest (Section~\ref{sec:data-limits}). Most of the teacher's real-image gain comes from these labels: the teacher without
corrections (uncorrected teacher) reaches \ControlThreeDPW\,mm PVE on converted 3DPW against \BaseThreeDPW\,mm
for the initial model, the teacher \FinalThreeDPW\,mm (Section~\ref{sec:res-3dpw}). On real photographs the
teacher separates children from adults better than the initial model (AUC \RHAUCBase{} to \RHAUCFinal) and ranks
women above men (gender AUC \RHGenderAUCFinal), but men receive mid-range gender values, probably because the AnnyOne
labels contain no fully male body (Sections~\ref{sec:res-rh} and~\ref{sec:res-mask}).

\paragraph{Compact reconstruction.}
The student has \ParamRatioStudent{} times fewer parameters and \FlopRatioStudent{} times fewer FLOPs than its
teacher and ran \SpeedRatioStudent{} times faster. Its PVE is \StudentPVE{} against \FinalPVE\,mm on the AnnyOne
holdout and \StudentThreeDPW{} against \FinalThreeDPW\,mm on converted 3DPW, missing the pre-set 95\,\% retention thresholds on every error metric (Section~\ref{sec:res-student}). Its 3DPW PVE is lower than the released baseline's
(\NAVERThreeDPW\,mm) and its PA-PVE slightly higher (\StudentPAThreeDPW{} against \NAVERPAThreeDPW\,mm); this
compares trained systems, not architectures (Section~\ref{sec:res-fairness}).

What distillation contributes remains unanswered. The ablation of Section~\ref{sec:res-kd-ablation}, which trains
the same ViT-B model on the same data and schedule without the distillation losses and without any teacher, had not
completed at submission. The student's accuracy can therefore not be attributed to distillation rather than to the
initialisation, the copied heads or the real-image data.

Training length and temperature were fixed in advance, and the sensitivity runs changed no setting
(Section~\ref{sec:res-selection}). Doubling the training length lowered the 3DPW validation PVE only from
\SelThirtyValPVE{} to \SelSixtyValPVE\,mm and the holdout PVE to \StudentSixtyPVE\,mm, without improving the 3DPW
test PVE (\StudentSixtyThreeDPW\,mm; Section~\ref{sec:res-training-length}). Across temperatures from 1 to 8 the 3DPW
test PVE differed by at most \KDTPRange\,mm, which single runs cannot separate from training variation
(Section~\ref{sec:res-kd-temperature}); validation values of the sweep are not reported. Untested causes of the gap are the smaller backbone, the 3DPW share per
batch and the teacher's 3DPW error.

\paragraph{Multi-person detection.}
For the teacher, recall on Relative Human falls with the number of people per image: \CrowdFinalOneRec\,\% and
\CrowdFinalTwoRec\,\% with one and two labelled people, \CrowdFinalThreeFourRec\,\% for three or four,
\CrowdFinalFiveEightRec\,\% for five to eight and \CrowdFinalNinePlusRec\,\% for nine or more
(Section~\ref{sec:res-multiperson}). The student was not evaluated on Relative Human, and its
recall in crowded images remains unmeasured.

\paragraph{Video.}
On the walking clip the student kept a tracked body in \VideoStudentMatched{} of \VideoFrames{} frames
and the released baseline in \VideoBaselineMatched{} (tracker matches, not detection recall;
Section~\ref{sec:res-video}).
Temporal filtering visibly steadied the motion and landmark alignment improved position and apparent size; without
ground truth this shows steadier output, not better 3D accuracy. On one \VideoGPU{}, model inference ran at
\VideoStudentRawFPS{} FPS for the student and \VideoNAVERFPS\,FPS for the released baseline, and the student with
preprocessing, filtering and alignment at \VideoAlignedPipeFPS\,FPS, excluding offline landmark extraction,
rendering and video input and output; these are not end-to-end speeds.

\section{Interpreting geometry and placement}
\label{sec:disc-pve}
\label{sec:disc-engineering}

PVE aligns the pelvises before measuring vertex distances and is therefore insensitive to placement: with the two
corrections the root error fell from \num{\ControlRootErr} to \FinalRootErr\,mm, while the PVE changed only from
\ControlPVE{} to \FinalPVE\,mm (Section~\ref{sec:res-depth}). Local changes are diluted likewise; the neck-skin PVE
fell from \ControlNeckSkin{} to \FinalNeckSkin\,mm, but the \DiscNeckVerts{} neck vertices account for an estimated
\DiscNeckPVEShare\,mm of whole-body PVE, so the bone correction was judged by bone angles and neck geometry
(Appendix~\ref{ch:diagnostics}). Detection, accuracy, stability and speed are therefore
reported separately.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:disc-limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Evaluation data.} The synthetic test set is the last \MethodHoldoutN{} AnnyOne images, without
  children, which also served as the in-training monitor; it supports no broad generalisation claim. The 3DPW labels
  are converted, and the 3DPW errors are not comparable with published results (Section~\ref{sec:data-limits}). The
  temperature analysis rests on test-set values only (Section~\ref{sec:res-sensitivity}).
\item \textbf{Baseline fairness.} No baseline was trained on the same data, so a lower PVE does not show a better
  architecture (Section~\ref{sec:res-fairness}).
\item \textbf{Distillation ablation.} No ViT-B model trained without distillation on the same data and schedule was
  evaluated, and the student's loss terms were not switched off one at a time, so the contribution of distillation is
  not established (Section~\ref{sec:res-kd-ablation}).
\item \textbf{Single runs.} Each model was trained once without a fixed seed (Section~\ref{sec:method-variation}).
\item \textbf{Shape.} Shape on real photographs was evaluated on the teacher only; the student, the deployed model,
  was not evaluated. The child--adult separation improves but remains unreliable, and the predicted gender of men
  stays near \RHMenGenderFinal{} (Section~\ref{sec:res-rh}).
\item \textbf{Depth.} Predicted depth grows by only about \FinalSlope\,m per metre of true depth, and a single image leaves
  size and distance ambiguous (Section~\ref{sec:res-depth}).
\item \textbf{Multi-person and video evaluation.} Multi-person evaluation is limited to detection recall, with no
  occlusion or crowd benchmark with 3D labels; the video is one clip of one person.
\item \textbf{Timing.} The forward-pass benchmark and the video timings were measured differently, and latency
  depends on hardware, resolution and the number of people; no real-time claim is made.
\item \textbf{Reproducibility.} The cluster scripts contain site-specific paths, and the terms of use of datasets,
  body model and checkpoints were not checked for a release of weights or labels.
\end{itemize}
