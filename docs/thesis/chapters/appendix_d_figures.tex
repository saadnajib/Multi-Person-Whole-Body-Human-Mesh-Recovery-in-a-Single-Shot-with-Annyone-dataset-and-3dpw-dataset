\chapter{Extra Figures}
\label{app:figures}

This appendix holds the figures that the chapters refer to but do not show: the second depth example, two more
bone-correction pairs, a second neck pair on 3DPW, the other demo frames and photos, all twelve demo images with
predicted fingers and nine more rest-pose line-ups. Every panel is judged by eye; where a body is placed from the
detector box, depth errors are hidden.
