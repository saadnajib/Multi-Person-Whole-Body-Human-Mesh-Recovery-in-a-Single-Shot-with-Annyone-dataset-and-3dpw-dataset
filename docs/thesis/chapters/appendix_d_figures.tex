\chapter{Extra figures}
\label{app:figures}

The chapters refer to the figures below but do not show them, so that the main text stays short. This appendix has
both depth examples and two more bone-correction pairs on the holdout. It also has a second neck pair on 3DPW, the
other demo frames and photos, and the in-training curves of Final and Control. The last two figures show all twelve
demo images with predicted fingers and nine more rest-pose line-ups.

I judged every image panel by eye, so none of them is a measurement. Where a body is placed from the detector box,
a wrong depth cannot be seen in the picture.
