\label{app:figures}

\captionsetup[figure]{justification=raggedright}% long file names in captions

This appendix collects the figures to which the main text refers without showing them. The models are the initial
model (Base), the teacher (Final), the teacher with full shape mask (Final-masked) and two pilots of
Appendix~\ref{app:pilots}: the depth pilot and the neck pilot. Several images print the run names recorded during
the project: ``v5'' and \path{anny_s2_shape_v5_00099} denote the initial model, ``final model (exp7)'' and
\path{exp7_v5_final_00009} the teacher (Table~\ref{tab:names}). All image panels were judged visually and none is a
measurement. Where a body is placed from the detector box (the demo setting), a wrong depth is not visible in the
picture.

\section{Depth, bone correction and in-training curves}
\label{app:figures-depth}

Figures~\ref{fig:depth-579862} and~\ref{fig:depth-579860} render two synthetic holdout images at each model's own
predicted depth. The depth pilot uses head-joint targets and a depth weight of $\times$5 for 10 epochs on AnnyOne
only. In image 579862 the predicted body grows from 0.76 to 0.95 of its true height; in image 579860 the initial
model draws a shrunken body too far away, so the ground-truth head shows above it. Both are single cases and not
typical ones.

Figure~\ref{fig:exp6-compare} adds two holdout pairs to Figure~\ref{fig:diag-neck-base}. In each image the top row
is the initial model as trained and the bottom row the same checkpoint with the bone correction applied to its
output, with no further training (Section~\ref{sec:diag-neck}). The columns are the input, the ground truth and the
prediction rendered in three evaluation settings, labelled in the image. The bodies are drawn at the model's own
predicted depth, so the depth defect still draws them too small; no metric exists for the necks.

\section{Demo images: initial model and teacher}
\label{app:figures-demo}

The demo images below compare the initial model with the teacher or with the neck pilot (the bone correction
followed by a 2-epoch fine-tune of the initial model). Each montage shows the photo and the first model's
prediction, then the photo and the second model's prediction. Bodies are placed from the detector box and, unless
stated otherwise, the fingers are set to rest pose. In the neck-pilot figures the two columns differ by the
correction and also by the fine-tune. The first four 3DPW test frames of the demo are in
Figure~\ref{fig:3dpw-overlays}; the tennis photo also appears in Figure~\ref{fig:real-photos}.

Figures~\ref{fig:fingers-all} to~\ref{fig:fingers-all-cont-b} show all twelve demo images with the predicted fingers
(not set to rest pose). The initial model still has the flipped base bones of the left index finger
(metacarpal1.L) and of the right ring finger; the teacher has the correction. In most images the hands are only a
few pixels wide and cannot be judged, so Figure~\ref{fig:fingers-demo} enlarges the three images where they can.

Figures~\ref{fig:rest-more} and~\ref{fig:rest-more-cont} show the other nine demo images in rest pose: every detected
person stands in the rest pose, so only the body shape differs between the models. Only the three render panels of
each montage are shown, enlarged; the photo is visible behind the bodies. Only large differences in build can be
seen. The other three images are in Figure~\ref{fig:rest-lineup}.
