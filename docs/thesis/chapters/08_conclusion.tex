\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

I moved Multi-HMR to the Anny body model and trained it on synthetic AnnyOne images and on real 3DPW images that I
converted to Anny. The model I started from had three visible failures. I first tested what was not their cause and
then traced each one to a mechanism: training targets built at the pelvis while the network anchors each person at
the head, and four bones turned by half a turn at the output. The fixes are small, and their effect is large where it
can be measured. Against a control with the same training, Final cut the root error from \num{\ControlRootErr} to
\FinalRootErr\,mm, the neck02 error from \ControlNeckTwo{} to \FinalNeckTwo\,degrees and the finger error from
\ControlFingers{} to \FinalFingers\,degrees, while the holdout PVE (\FinalPVE{} against \ControlPVE\,mm) stayed within
run-to-run variation. The main lesson is about measurement: the standard error measure could not see the worst
defect, and each failure became tractable only once a number for it existed.

In the order of the supervisor's requirements: for the pose (1a), Final has \FinalThreeDPW\,mm PVE on the 3DPW test
set against our labels, with necks and fingers corrected on the holdout; on real photos the pose is judged by eye,
and predicted fingers, shown in Figure~\ref{fig:fingers-demo}, look like hands in Final and are splayed and stiff
in Base on the images where the hands are large enough to judge. For the shape (1a), Final recognises gender in
\RHGenderFinal\,\% of the Relative Human people (Base \RHGenderBase\,\%) and separates children from adults with an
AUC of \RHAUCFinal{} (Base \RHAUCBase); children are a measured improvement, not solved, and muscle and weight barely
vary between people on real photos. For 1b, the Student has \ParamRatioStudent$\times$ fewer parameters and
\FlopRatioStudent$\times$ fewer FLOPs than Final, but its accuracy was not yet measured, so 1b is not met at the time
of writing; NAVER's Multi-HMR-Anny, measured with our code, reaches \NAVERThreeDPW\,mm on 3DPW. For 1c and 2a, the
converted 3DPW labels exist with three defects fixed and an independent check, and training on them brought the 3DPW
error from \BaseThreeDPW{} (Base) to \ControlThreeDPW\,mm (Control) before any fix. Motion in video (2b) was not
started. For 3, every number traces to a run, a log and a checkpoint (Appendix~\ref{app:reproduction}); the code is not
yet cleaned up or released. Every model was trained once without a fixed seed, and the 3DPW numbers are not comparable
with published 3DPW results; Section~\ref{sec:disc-limitations} lists the limitations.

\section{Future work}
\label{sec:future}

The steps below are planned, each with its state on 18 October 2026.

\paragraph{The Student's result (1b).} The Student was training on that date, in a single job of
\MethodStudentEpochs{} epochs. Its result is expected within days of the job's start and fills the table of
Section~\ref{sec:res-student}. If it misses the 95\,\% bar while its error is still falling, I will continue its
training from the last checkpoint and report the ratio as measured. Which model sets the bar, Final or
Multi-HMR-Anny, is the supervisor's decision.

\paragraph{The partial shape mask (1a), done.} Final-partial-mask, which masks the seven constant phenotypes and keeps age
supervised, finished on 19 October: it kept the children's gain (AUC \RHAUCPartial) but lost the gender gain
(\RHGenderPartial\,\%), so the unmasked Final stays the final model and the Student's teacher
(Section~\ref{sec:res-mask}).

\paragraph{Evidence that shape follows the person (1a).} The rest-pose line-up (Figure~\ref{fig:rest-lineup}) and
the demo with predicted fingers (Figure~\ref{fig:fingers-demo}) are done, by eye only. Still planned is a number:
per-phenotype correlation, slope and $R^2$ between prediction and label on the AnnyOne holdout, from per-person files
that already exist.

\paragraph{A measured spread for Final.} A second run of Final with the same recipe would replace ``within run-to-run
variation'' by a measured spread. The training code sets no seed, so this is a repeat, not a seeded run. It takes
about two GPU hours and is planned for the fourth week if a slot is free.

\paragraph{Motion in video (2b).} In the second month I plan a small temporal refiner on the frozen per-frame model.
It reads the per-person tokens of Final over windows of 16 frames (1.6\,s at the 10 frames per second of the labels)
with a two-layer transformer and pools the body shape over each track. It is trained on the 3DPW training tracks and
judged by acceleration error and PVE against the per-frame model, shape averaging and temporal smoothing. If it does
not beat smoothing, the thesis will say so.

\paragraph{Code and model release (3).} The code will get one script that reproduces the evaluations, paths set by
environment variables, an environment file and a rewritten README. Final, Final-masked, Control, Base and the Student
will be released with checksums, subject to the licence terms of AnnyOne, NAVER's checkpoints and 3DPW.

\paragraph{Open beyond this plan.} Two problems have no plan inside this thesis. Children still receive adult-shaped
bodies too often; weak age labels from the Relative Human training split or child-rich synthetic data are candidates.
The depth slope of \FinalSlope{} means that near and far people still get similar depths; the likely reason, a pull
toward the typical training distance, was not tested.
