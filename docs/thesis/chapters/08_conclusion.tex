\chapter{Conclusion and future work}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

This thesis addressed multi-person whole-body mesh recovery from a single image in the Anny body model at reduced
inference cost, for which no real images with Anny labels existed. The solution has four parts: 3DPW's SMPL
labels were converted to Anny by joint-based fitting with virtual torso joints; a ViT-L teacher was trained on
AnnyOne and converted 3DPW with consistent head anchors and a fixed bone rotation correction; a ViT-B student
inherited the teacher's heads through a learned projector and was distilled from its features and outputs; and
temporal filtering and landmark alignment were added for video at inference time.

The student has \ParamRatioStudent{} times fewer parameters than its teacher and ran \SpeedRatioStudent{} times faster, but its PVE is \StudentPVE{} against \FinalPVE\,mm on the
AnnyOne holdout and \StudentThreeDPW{} against \FinalThreeDPW\,mm on converted 3DPW, so it misses the retention
criterion set in advance. Its 3DPW PVE is below the \NAVERThreeDPW\,mm of the released baseline, which was trained on
different data. Neither longer training nor another distillation temperature closed the gap.
The teacher's detection recall on Relative Human falls from \CrowdFinalOneRec--\CrowdFinalTwoRec\,\% for one or two people
per image to \CrowdFinalNinePlusRec\,\% for nine or more. On video, filtering and alignment produced visibly
steadier and better placed output, and the student's model inference ran at \VideoStudentRawFPS{} against
\VideoNAVERFPS\,FPS for the baseline.

Unresolved are the cause of the accuracy gap and the share of it that distillation recovers, body shape on real
photographs (children, men), the compression of predicted depth, and a learned temporal model; the evaluation is
bounded by a small synthetic holdout, single runs and the absence of a fairly trained baseline
(Section~\ref{sec:disc-limitations}).

\section{Future work}
\label{sec:future}

\paragraph{Temporal evaluation and modelling.}
Running the filter and the alignment on the 3DPW test sequences, whose converted labels exist at \DataLabelFps{}
frames per second, would measure steadiness and per-frame PVE together; clips with occlusion, a moving camera or
several people, timed end to end, would follow. A learned temporal model (Section~\ref{sec:bg-temporal}) is a
separate project.

\paragraph{Accuracy at reduced cost.}
Other distillation loss weights, other forms of feature transfer and more real images per batch are the next
candidates for narrowing the gap. A released baseline fine-tuned on the same converted 3DPW data would separate the
effect of architecture and distillation from that of the training data.

\paragraph{Real-image shape.}
Age-group labels, body measurements or further Anny fits on real photographs could replace the constant phenotypes.
Tests with children should separate whether Anny can represent child proportions from whether the network
recognises a child in a photograph.

\paragraph{Reliability and reproducibility.}
Repeated runs with uncertainty estimates and a larger, randomly drawn holdout that includes children would show
which differences are real and how far the results generalise. A portable configuration with release checks would
make the system easier to verify.
