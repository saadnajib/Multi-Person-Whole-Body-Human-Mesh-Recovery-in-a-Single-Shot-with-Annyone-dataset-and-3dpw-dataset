\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This thesis addresses multi-person whole-body human mesh recovery from a single image in the Anny body model, whose
named shape parameters include age and height, at reduced inference cost. No real images with Anny labels exist,
and the large Vision Transformer encoders of accurate single-shot models are expensive.

For real-image supervision, the SMPL labels of 3DPW are converted to Anny by fitting corresponding joints, giving
\DataThreeDPWTotalImages{} images with \DataThreeDPWTotalPeople{} labelled people. A ViT-L teacher based on
Multi-HMR is trained on synthetic AnnyOne images and converted 3DPW with two corrections: head-anchor targets
consistent with the decoder and a fixed rotation correction of four output bones. A ViT-B student inherits the
teacher's prediction heads through a learned projector and is distilled from the teacher's features and outputs.
For video, temporal filtering and alignment to detected 2D landmarks run at inference without retraining.

The student has \ParamRatioStudent{} times fewer parameters and \FlopRatioStudent{} times fewer FLOPs than the
teacher and runs \SpeedRatioStudent{} times faster, but is less accurate: its per-vertex error (PVE) is
\StudentPVE\,mm on held-out AnnyOne images and \StudentThreeDPW\,mm on converted 3DPW, against \FinalPVE{} and
\FinalThreeDPW\,mm for the teacher. On 3DPW its PVE is lower than that of the released Multi-HMR-Anny checkpoint
(the released baseline, \NAVERThreeDPW\,mm), which was not trained on the converted labels. Neither longer training
nor another distillation temperature closes the gap to the teacher. The teacher's detection recall on real
photographs falls with the number of people per image (\CrowdFinalTwoRec\,\% with two, \CrowdFinalNinePlusRec\,\%
with nine or more); the student was not evaluated there. On a walking video, filtering and alignment visibly steady
the output and improve its placement, a qualitative result without ground truth.

The synthetic test set is a small holdout of \MethodHoldoutN{} images without children, the real-image labels are
converted rather than measured, every model was trained once, and the contribution of distillation was not isolated
by an ablation. Body shape on real photographs was analysed for the teacher only, which separates children from
adults better than the initial model but not reliably.
