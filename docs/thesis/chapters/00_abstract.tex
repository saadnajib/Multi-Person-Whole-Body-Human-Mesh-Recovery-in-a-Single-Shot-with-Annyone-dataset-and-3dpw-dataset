\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This thesis addresses multi-person whole-body human mesh recovery from a single image in the Anny body model, whose
named shape parameters include age and height, at reduced inference cost. No real images with Anny labels exist,
and the large Vision Transformer encoders of accurate single-shot models are expensive.

For real-image supervision, the SMPL labels of 3DPW are converted to Anny by fitting corresponding joints and
virtual torso joints, giving \DataThreeDPWTotalImages{} images with \DataThreeDPWTotalPeople{} labelled people.
A ViT-L teacher based on Multi-HMR is trained on synthetic AnnyOne images and converted 3DPW, with two corrections to
its targets: consistent head anchors and a fixed bone rotation correction. A ViT-B student inherits the teacher's
prediction heads through a learned projector and is distilled from the teacher's features and outputs. For video,
temporal filtering and alignment to detected 2D landmarks run at inference without retraining.

The student has \ParamRatioStudent{} times fewer parameters and \FlopRatioStudent{} times fewer FLOPs than the
teacher and runs \SpeedRatioStudent{} times faster, but is less accurate: its per-vertex error (PVE) is
\StudentPVE\,mm on held-out AnnyOne images and \StudentThreeDPW\,mm on converted 3DPW, against \FinalPVE{} and
\FinalThreeDPW\,mm for the teacher. On 3DPW its PVE is lower than the released Multi-HMR-Anny baseline's
(\NAVERThreeDPW\,mm), which was not trained on the converted labels. Neither longer training nor another
distillation temperature closes the gap to the teacher. Detection recall on real photographs falls with the number of people per image. On a walking video, filtering and alignment visibly steady the output and improve its
placement, a qualitative result without ground truth.

The synthetic test set is a small holdout of \MethodHoldoutN{} images without children, the real-image labels are
converted rather than measured, and every model was trained once. Body shape on real photographs was analysed for
the teacher only, which separates children from adults better than the initial model but not reliably.
