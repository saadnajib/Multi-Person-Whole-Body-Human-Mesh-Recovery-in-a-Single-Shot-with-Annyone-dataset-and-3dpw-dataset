\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This thesis addresses multi-person whole-body human mesh recovery from a single image in the Anny body model, whose
named shape parameters include age, at reduced inference cost. No real images with Anny labels exist,
and the large Vision Transformer encoders of accurate single-shot models are expensive.

For real-image supervision, 3DPW's SMPL labels are converted to Anny by fitting corresponding joints. A ViT-L teacher based on
Multi-HMR is trained on synthetic AnnyOne images and converted 3DPW with corrected head-anchor targets
and a fixed rotation correction of four output bones. A ViT-B student inherits the
teacher's prediction heads through a learned projector and is distilled from the teacher's features and outputs.
For video, temporal filtering and alignment to detected 2D landmarks run at inference time.

The student has \ParamRatioStudent{} times fewer parameters and \FlopRatioStudent{} times fewer FLOPs than the
teacher and runs \SpeedRatioStudent{} times faster, but is less accurate: its per-vertex error (PVE) is
\StudentPVE\,mm on held-out AnnyOne images and \StudentThreeDPW\,mm on converted 3DPW, against \FinalPVE{} and
\FinalThreeDPW\,mm for the teacher. On 3DPW its PVE is lower than that of the released Multi-HMR-Anny checkpoint
(the released baseline, \NAVERThreeDPW\,mm), which was not trained on the converted labels. Neither longer training
nor another distillation temperature closes the gap. An ablation finds no measurable effect of the
distillation losses, whereas training with the transferred teacher heads lowers the 3DPW PVE by \AblNoTeachDiffTP\,mm.
The teacher's detection recall on real photographs falls with crowd size (\CrowdFinalTwoRec\,\% with two, \CrowdFinalNinePlusRec\,\%
with nine or more). On a walking video, filtering and alignment visibly steady
the output and improve its placement (qualitative, without ground truth).

The synthetic test set is a small holdout of \MethodHoldoutN{} images without children, the real-image labels are
converted rather than measured, and every model was trained once. Crowd recall and body shape on real photographs were analysed for the teacher only,
which separates children from adults better than the initial model but not reliably.
