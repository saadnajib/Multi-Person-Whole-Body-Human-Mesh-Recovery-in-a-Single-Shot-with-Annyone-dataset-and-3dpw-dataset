\chapter{Reproduction: scripts and flags}
\label{app:scripts}

Every experiment in this thesis is a Slurm job script in the project
repository; the table lists the ones behind the reported numbers. All of them
run \texttt{train.py}, \texttt{smpl\_to\_anny.py} or \texttt{demo.py} with
command-line flags only, so any row of any table can be re-run without
editing code.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{p{4.6cm}p{8.6cm}}
\toprule
Script & Produces \\
\midrule
\texttt{train\_step1\_pretrained\_bb.sh}, \texttt{train\_step2\_frozen\_bb.sh} & teacher stages 1--2 (Table~\ref{tab:main}) \\
\texttt{train\_s2\_partialft.sh}, \texttt{train\_s2\_boosted\_v4.sh}, \texttt{train\_s2\_shape\_v5.sh} & teacher stages 3--5; \texttt{anny\_s2\_shape\_v5} is the teacher \\
\texttt{run\_eval\_test.sh} & four-metric evaluation on the Anny-One holdout \\
\texttt{train\_distill\_vits.sh} \ldots \texttt{\_v6.sh}, \texttt{\_v6b.sh}, \texttt{\_v6c.sh} & students v1--v6c (Tables~\ref{tab:student_runs}--\ref{tab:kd_eval}) \\
\texttt{train\_distill\_vits\_v7\_mixed.sh} & mixed synthetic--real student (Section~\ref{sec:mixed}) \\

\label{app:mapping}

The correspondence used by the final converter, with the rest-pose offset of
each virtual joint (Section~\ref{sec:smpl2anny}). Offsets are measured on the
two rest skeletons after aligning them at the hip midpoint and scaling SMPL to
Anny's hip--neck length (factor 0.864); limb joints use the bone head.
